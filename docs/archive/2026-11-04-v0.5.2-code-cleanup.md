## Rejected and deferred

- No product-module deletion met the admission bar. Shared lifecycle,
  allocation, verification and cancellation paths have distinct trust or
  failure semantics. Reconsider only with an admitted module rework or a
  demonstrated duplicate, not a new abstraction or smaller syntax alone.
- Profile-comment cuts would change the bundled fingerprint without a
  recipe change. Reconsider alongside a necessary profile update.
- The `getAuthStatus` revisit trigger has not fired in pinned Codex
  0.166.0. No other revisit trigger was established as fired.
- #264's four builds serve different retention boundaries. No safe cut
  established a remedy for its 336.12-second ordinary CI run. Adam chose
  to defer that additional coverage; `ce76d84` removes it from this release.
  Its draft PR and failed budget evidence remain. No waiver or retry.
- The bounded Apple lifecycle investigation supports no product fix;
  #167, #202 and #207 remain open with qualified evidence.
- Auxiliary source review found that `scripts/e2e-linux.sh` could select an
  older dispatch when called again for the same commit. This was not
  reproduced through the CLI and no fix was admitted. Release validation
  uses directly recorded run IDs, not this helper. Reconsider separately
  with a reproduction and a unique invocation or exact dispatch identity.
